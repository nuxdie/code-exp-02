# Repository instructions

Work directly on `main`. Do not create branches or worktrees.

Each completed chunk of work must be committed and pushed to `main` before starting
the next chunk or handing the work back to the user.

Keep UI copy and user-facing explanations concise. Avoid persistent shortcut hints,
obvious instructions, and repeated explanatory text. Put detailed usage guidance
in help, and retain details needed to understand errors or consequential actions.
